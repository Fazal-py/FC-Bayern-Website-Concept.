The Club

We are not here to take part.

We are here to win everything, and then to win it again.

Mia san mia. We are who we are — and everyone who pulls on the red already knows exactly what that costs.

Founded in Munich in 1900, FC Bayern has grown into the most decorated club in German football — and one of the most followed institutions in world sport.

The standard is set on the red carpet of the Allianz Arena, where more than 75,000 arrive expecting nothing less than the title, every single season.

Red and white, with the blue-and-white Bavarian diamonds at its heart. Ambition worn on the chest. That is the whole idea.
